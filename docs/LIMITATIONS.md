# Limitations

- This is preliminary document understanding, not legal advice. It must not be used to decide whether a clause or agreement is legal, valid, or enforceable, to predict a case outcome, or to decide whether to sign.
- The intended scope is rental and employment agreements supplied as PDFs or plain-text files. Tesseract OCR is used locally for image-containing PDF pages with little selectable text, but OCR can misread names, numbers, punctuation, or legal wording. Check OCR-extracted passages against the original document. Scanned PDFs require Tesseract and its English language data to be installed.
- Model-generated summaries, explanations, interpretations and concern flags may be incomplete or mistaken. Citation checking confirms only that a quoted passage exists in the referenced clause; it does not prove that an explanation follows from the quote.
- BM25 is lexical retrieval and may miss relevant clauses phrased differently from the question.
- Risk checks are simple heuristics and can produce false positives or miss issues. Absence flags describe only phrases that the app did not find; they do not establish that a document lacks a legal requirement.
- Local model quality and latency depend on installed model files and device hardware. Responses can be slow, malformed, or unavailable if Ollama is stopped.
- The prototype does not identify jurisdiction or state applicable law. Local law may affect topics such as notice periods, deposits, non-competes, penalties, stamp duty and registration.
- Generic jurisdiction background warnings are deterministic keyword/regular-expression matches over extracted clause text. They are fixed text, do not come from a document or external data, and do not identify the applicable jurisdiction or explain what a rule requires. They may miss relevant wording or over-trigger on unrelated wording. A warning is not a finding about a clause.
- Optional statutory background is available only when a user manually builds a local subset from Open India Law. Its curated rule-to-title and section-title mapping may miss relevant material or retrieve a mismatch; the starter Act list is small and must be reviewed. Source titles contain inconsistencies and typos, which can affect matching. A displayed excerpt is verbatim source text, not an interpretation, and the point-in-time snapshot may be outdated or amended. Verify linked source material with a qualified local professional. If the local subset is absent or cannot be loaded, statutory background is disabled.
- Sessions exist only in backend memory, expire after the configured TTL, and are lost on server restart. This is not durable or encrypted document storage.
