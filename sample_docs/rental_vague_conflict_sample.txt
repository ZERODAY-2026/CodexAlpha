FICTIONAL RENTAL AGREEMENT ADDENDUM

1. Rent. The tenant must pay rent of $1,250 on the first day of every month.

2. Repairs. The landlord may require reasonable repairs, as decided by the landlord, and the tenant must complete them promptly.

3. Ending the agreement. Either party may end this agreement by giving 30 days' written notice.

4. Early ending. The tenant must give 60 days' notice before ending the tenancy.

5. Deposit. The tenant pays a security deposit of $1,250.
